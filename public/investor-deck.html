The problem

Enterprises are flying blind
on their biggest new risk.

Data walks out the door

Employees paste secrets, customer data, and IP into AI tools. Once it's sent, it's gone — no recall, no record.

No policy, no control

No enforced rules on who uses which model for what. IT's only lever today is a blanket "no" — which nobody obeys.

Cost sprawl

Separate licenses per provider, runaway token spend, zero visibility. AI budgets balloon with no accountability.

The choice today is block AI and lose the productivity, or allow it and lose the data. Both are wrong.
